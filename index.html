Silver, Made to Be Treasured

Sterling silver is genuine silver, commonly made with 92.5% silver and 7.5% other metals to give it added strength and durability.

Silver’s bright lustre, fine finish and timeless appeal make it a beautiful choice for traditional articles, serving pieces and special occasions.
